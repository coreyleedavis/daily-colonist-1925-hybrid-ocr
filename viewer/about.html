About this index — The Daily Colonist, 1925: hybrid OCR construction notes

Corpus and outputs

312 issues, 6,641 indexed pages, 26,891,630 words. Source scans ~7,500×9,500px grayscale PNGs (decoded from JP2). Deliverable per page: MiniOCR XML (word-level bounding boxes in full-resolution pixel space, searchable alternatives inline) plus a provenance sidecar (per-word production class, emit-order aligned). Index: Apache Solr 9 with the solr-ocrhighlighting plugin — documents store file pointers; the plugin tokenizes at index time and computes highlight geometry at query time from the matched words' coordinates.

Input streams

(1) Tesseract 5, TSV output: word-level boxes and per-word confidence 0–100 in full-resolution space. Confidence empirically honest (garbage scores low), but error modes are structural rather than typographic: silent region dropout (up to ~40% of some pages), display-type shattering into fragment tokens, systematic misreads, and — ink-verified — glyph confabulation at high confidence (¢ emitted on plain-c printing at conf 58–88). (2) PaddleOCR-VL 1.6 served via vLLM (OpenAI-compatible endpoint): block-segmented JSON with labels (text, title, table, image…), block boxes in 2,560px space, no confidence reporting, markedly higher transcription accuracy. Per-page scale factors between coordinate spaces computed fresh from page dimensions (the ratio drifts page to page).

Synthesis pipeline

Sanitize (table-HTML stripping, Unicode normalization, astral-plane removal) → conservative block dedup (exact containment and hyphen-stub continuation only; fuzzy matching rejected after false-positive analysis on shipping tables) → regroup (each Tesseract word assigned to the VLM block containing its center point; image-labeled blocks excluded from text; unassigned words become rescue candidates) → character-level alignment per block (difflib; size-guarded) mapping VLM words onto Tesseract geometry: unique alignment inherits the measured box, partial alignment against a fused token gets a proportional slice, multi-word alignment gets the union box, no alignment gets a line-proportional interpolated box (39.1% of the corpus — a direct measure of Tesseract dropout). A shrapnel rule refuses alignment charity when one fragment token is claimed by multiple VLM words at gross length mismatch.

Disagreement routing cascade: punctuation-normalized identity → Tesseract confidence <50 → truncation (Tesseract reading a substring of the VLM's) → external-dictionary vote (system wordlist — not the corpus lexicon; see limitations) → residual cases indexed under both readings at the same token position via the OCR plugin's alternatives mechanism (~498K positions). Orphan rescue: adaptive 2D agglomerative clustering (distance scaled to local type size), confidence gating with a no-alphanumeric rejection rule, emitted as tess-only blocks spliced into the VLM's reading order by column-flow geometry (block sequence corpus-verified at 0.022% geometric violations over 926K pairs). Cross-seam dehyphenation with dictionary confirmation. Twelve provenance classes; corpus distribution: agree 35.7%, interp 39.1%, vlm-routed 6.6%, tess-only 4.6%, punct 3.5%, others <3% each.

LLM arbitration: built, evaluated, not deployed

The ~58K numeric-band disagreements (no dictionary vote possible) were extracted with crop geometry for judge-model arbitration. Evaluation on a 16-crop instrumented set: Qwen2.5-VL-7B under an A/B choice protocol showed 14/16 position bias under label swap; under blind transcription it hallucinated currency framings on illegible crops. qwen3-vl (8B/32B, thinking disabled) was honest — zero hallucinations, abstained on illegible input — but confirmed the indexed reading in every verdict (yield: zero corrections). MiniCPM-V 4.5's only two index-changing verdicts were both wrong against ink; DeepSeek-OCR confabulated document idioms (invented equations) on out-of-distribution crops. Human verification of every resolvable disputed crop (7/7) found the VLM's indexed reading correct — including a case where the "ground truth" instrument itself was wrong and Tesseract had confabulated the glyph. The numeric band therefore ships VLM-primary and unarbitrated, with measured justification; the harness and acceptance protocol (verify instruments against ink first) are archived judge-agnostic.

Comparative statistics

Identical methodology across arms (double-HTML-unescape, alphanumeric normalization, ≥4-char tokens checked against the corpus-derived 1925 lexicon at frequency ≥3): Tesseract 19.60M words, 747,995 unique forms, 92.76% recognized; VLM 26.78M, 256,718, 97.41%; hybrid 26.89M, 341,484, 97.21%. Note the unique-forms signal: Tesseract's 748K vocabulary is dominated by damage (621,822 forms exclusive to it); the hybrid's 341K includes rescue-region contributions at a 0.2-point recognized-rate cost against the VLM.

Known limitations

Interpolated boxes are line-proportional estimates, not measurements. Highlight geometry carries up to ~0.7% scale error on pages where Tesseract's page dimensions differ from the served image's (fix queued: per-page canvas/TSV rescaling). The recognized-% metric uses the corpus lexicon, which demonstrably contains OCR-derived misspellings (in places outnumbering correct forms — the reason routing votes use an external dictionary); rates are cross-arm comparable, not absolute accuracy. Image descriptions (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) are indexed in a separate image_desc field, searched only in the labeled images mode, and are never counted as page text — the Phase 1 baseline conflated these, inflating text-search counts (e.g. railway: 2,717 conflated vs 2,636 ink-only). Low-confidence rescues are flagged (tess-only-lowconf) but retained for searchability.
